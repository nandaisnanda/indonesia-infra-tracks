Indonesia Toll Lines

Toll roads by year of opening, grouped by presidential term. Click a card to focus on one era, click a line to zoom to that toll road.

How to read: color shows when a toll section opened, not who planned or built it. Grey = opening date not found, section not yet split out, or not yet open.

Data: geometry © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); opening dates from Indonesian Wikipedia and news reports, not verified against BPJT. Map: Web Mercator, for exploration, not for comparing areas.
