Indonesia Infrastructure Lines

Toll roads, railways and national roads by year of opening, grouped by presidential term. Switch layers on or off, click a card to focus on one era, click a line to zoom to it.

How to read: color shows when a toll section opened, not who planned or built it. Grey = opening date not found. Railways and national roads without a date show as "existing" and are always visible.

Data: geometry © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); toll dates from Indonesian Wikipedia and news reports, not verified against BPJT. Rail dates come from OSM tags and a short curated list. Km: toll = carriageway ÷ 2, rail = track, roads = centerline. Map: Web Mercator, for exploration, not for comparing areas.
